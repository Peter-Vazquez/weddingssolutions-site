Peter Vazquez, Chaplain

Ordained chaplain, veteran, public speaker, and community leader serving couples throughout Rochester, Western New York, and the Finger Lakes.

The Person Standing With You

The ceremony is not about the officiant.

It is about the two people standing together, the promises they are making, and the family and friends who have come to witness that moment. My role is to help make that moment clear, personal, calm, and worthy of the commitment being made.

As an ordained chaplain, I approach weddings with respect for marriage, family, faith, and the individual values of each couple. Some couples want a traditional Christian ceremony. Some want a civil ceremony with no religious language. Others want something in between. The ceremony should sound like you, not like a script that could belong to anybody.

My background in public speaking, radio, executive leadership, military service, teaching, and community work has taught me how to lead important moments without making myself the center of them. On your wedding day, that means a steady voice, clear preparation, and a calm presence when the room needs one.

How I Approach the Ceremony

Prepared. Personal. Respectful.

The goal is not to make your ceremony complicated. It is to make sure the important parts are handled well.

Listen First

Your ceremony begins with understanding the tone, values, family, and meaning you want reflected.

Prepare Clearly

You should know what will happen, what you need to bring, and what is expected before the wedding day.

Lead Calmly

The ceremony should feel natural and confident, even when the wedding day around it feels busy.

Keep It Yours

Faith, tradition, family, service, culture, humor, and personal story can be included when they belong to you.
